01 / The Idea

Every community has a courtyard.

In Haitian tradition, the lakou is the courtyard where the whole community gathers — where people meet, talk, and take care of each other.

Lakou Labs builds that gathering place for your business online: one address where your customers find you, see your work, and reach you in a single touch — opening with motion, the way walking through your front door feels.

Found on GoogleYour site is set up with your Google Business Profile, so “near me” searches land on a page that works.
One-touch contactTap-to-call and your booking link, right up front — no hunting, no menus to dig through.
Motion, not a static pageYour site opens with a short entrance filmed in your real shop or built from your photos — not a stock template.
